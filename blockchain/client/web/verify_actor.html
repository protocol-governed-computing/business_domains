Record a Verification Decision

An authority decides about a registered person. Accepting and rejecting are two different acts, not one act with an outcome — which is why the business records them separately and offers them under separate names.

Whoever this address identifies is the person being decided about.
Recorded as stated. Nothing checks that you are this authority, or that this authority may decide.
A rejection states its grounds, because the grounds are the substance of the decision. An acceptance need not — the decision is itself the statement.
A person is decided about once. A second decision is refused by the business, which is not the same as being turned away at the door — the answer tells you which.
