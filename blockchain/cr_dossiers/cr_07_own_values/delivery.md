## What this change closed

**What happened before.** Each identity act recorded the moment and the trail the request named.
Registration claimed the address at the place the request named. Wallet wrote whatever record the
request supplied, including its holder and its balance, and the request chose the prefix of the
wallet's identity and the moment on its trail. The entrances sent these values, so callers through
an entrance saw nothing wrong, but a direct caller could create a wallet for another holder with any
balance.

**What happens now.** Each act states those values in its bindings. The wallet's holder is the person
the act resolved, the balance is zero, the denomination BACHI, the classification DEFAULT and the
state ACTIVE. A request still naming its own values is admitted, and none of them is used.

**Identity.**
- `WF_REGISTER_ACTOR_V2`, `WF_ACCEPT_ACTOR_V2`, `WF_REJECT_ACTOR_V2` and `WF_CREATE_WALLET_V2`
  replace the V1 acts cr_06 authored.
- `TI_REGISTER_ACTOR_V1`, `TI_ACCEPT_ACTOR_V1` and `TI_REJECT_ACTOR_V1` replace the entrances v5
  published.
- `IN_WALLET_CREATION_V1` replaces the wallet entry point v5 published.
- The three identity entry points are re-pointed to their act's successor.

**Retired by record** in `.github/process/retired_identities.yaml`:

- `WF_RECORD_VERIFICATION_DECISION_V0`, which named two of those, and `IN_ACTOR_VERIFIED_V0`, which
  starts it.
